## Where a claim is filed

Every FBA claim and case is opened, evidenced, answered and reopened by the seller, or a user the seller authorises, in Seller Central (FL1, SU12).
Amazon may decline a claim that skips its kind's process or withholds requested information, and opens one only for an item not already reimbursed (FL2, FL3).
Entry points, evidence, limits and answers by kind: references/filing.md.

## Conduct

Amazon names insufficiently researched or premature requests and many requests in a short time as conduct that can bring delayed support, monitoring or account action (FL11).
From 2026-03-04 the Business Solutions Agreement requires any automated agent accessing Amazon Services to identify itself, comply with the Agent Policy and cease access when Amazon asks (FL22).
Conduct, deactivations and third-party filers: references/conduct.md.

## What cx reads

The claim reads sit on Amazon Fulfillment, Pricing and Finance and Accounting; no notification announces a reimbursement or a case change (SU2, SU3, SU5, SU31).
Every read with its role, lag and look-back: references/reads.md.

## No door

No operation files a claim, opens or answers a case, attaches evidence, enters a sourcing cost, requests a remeasure or disputes a value; the Support API reads cases on a restricted role outside the app, and nothing is built on it (SU12, SU8).
Each step as the seller's step in Seller Central: references/no-door.md.

## Windows and stores

Each kind's window is a per-store length on Amazon's page for that kind and in the store's News posts (FC9, RT4, IN7, RM6).
For warehouse losses and inbound shortfalls no operation returns or states it (FC9, IN7).
Where each is stated, and a forum post's store: references/windows-and-stores.md.

## Recovery

Filing oneself, a commission service, a subscription or ERP tool, entering a sourcing cost or leaving a kind alone are the seller's choices (EC25).
The market and the amount owed as a formula: references/recovery.md.
