# Unsellable units, post-order losses, customer returns and the cap

## Unsellable units

- Pre-order units unsellable when lost, damaged or removed are reimbursed at "a reduced estimated cost"; Amazon may ask for documents to set it, and no page gives the reduction (VR25).
- Japan's texts state the reduced estimate for removal claims of unsellable items only (VR25).
- Post-order units unsellable when lost or damaged are reimbursed at "the discounted sale price of that order minus applicable fees", with VAT also out in the UK and DE (VR26).
- That post-order sentence was inserted in the 2024-12 to 2025-02 redlines for the US, CA, UK and DE; the JP texts of 2025-02-19 carry none (VR26).
- The self-service dispute workflow applies an approved cost to pre-order events reimbursed as "sellable", by US-store staff (VR21).

## Post-order losses and customer returns

- The US, CA, UK and DE redlines (2024-12 to 2025-02, rendered) and the JP English text strike "refund amount or the … of the replacement item" and insert "sales", reading "the sales price on the original order minus applicable fees" (VAT also out in the UK and DE; consumption tax in JP) (VR27).
- The US and CA Chinese texts insert "销售" only (VR27).
- The live *FBA inventory reimbursement policy* page is one the seller sees signed in, unread by a permitted route; which basis a store applies today, and whether a partial refund sets the value, is read in Amazon's documents, not yet seen on a live account (VR27).
- Japan's two texts of the same date differ: the English strikes the refund amount and replacement price, the Japanese keeps both and adds the original order's sales price beside them ("元の注文の販売価格、返金額または交換商品の価格から、適用される手数料と消費税を差し引いたもの") (VR42).
- A US-store seller (2026-08-05) reports a materially-different customer return reimbursed through removal handling at sourcing cost, below the buy cost, rather than at the order basis (VR33).
- Which customer returns are owed: ../return-claims/SKILL.md.

## The per-unit cap

- Amazon caps the reimbursement for a single unit, and the cap differs by store in amount and currency (VR29).
- The cap for a store is on *FBA inventory reimbursement policy*, which the seller sees signed in, and in Amazon's dated redline PDFs (2025-02-24; JP 2025-02-19), read by plain GET; no operation returns it (VR29).
- No cap was found in any text read for AU, MX, IN, BR or other stores (VR29).
- A line paid at the cap on a unit worth more is paid as the policy states (VR29).
