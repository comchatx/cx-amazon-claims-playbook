# Amazon claims playbook: research

This directory is the research base for a skill pack over `cx-amazon-sp-api`, ComChatX's open door to Amazon's Selling Partner API (`cx raw amazon-sp-api`). Its brief is 548 (the Amazon claims playbook): what Amazon owes a seller when FBA units are lost, damaged, short, not returned or wrongly charged; by when the seller may ask; what the money is worth; what a claim costs the seller in conduct; and what the paid recovery services sell. Nine research files, each written by a research seat, checked by an adversarial verifier, reviewed by a completeness critic and then revised by its seat, were read and revised on 2026-11-13. This page is the entry point; the files hold the evidence (quotations, column names, model pointers, dates and the pages behind them).

## 1. What the tree is, and how it was read

**The route rule.** Every page was read by a plain GET of a public page: Seller Forums threads, Amazon's PDFs on m.media-amazon.com, sell.amazon.*, supplychain.amazon.com, gs.amazon.cn, globalselling.amazon.com, developer-docs.amazon.com (and its `.md` twins), GitHub (`gh api` and the web) and third-party sites, plus a grep of a local clone of `amzn/selling-partner-api-models` at commit `db2936aa` (2026-10-29), checked against `main` at `b13d1d10` (2026-11-11, an Economics schema change only). Kept out, by rule: no Selling Partner API host, no `POST` to any Amazon host, no sign-in, no seller's data.

**No Seller Central help page was read first-hand.** A plain GET of `sellercentral.amazon.<tld>/help/hub/reference/external/<ID>` or `/gp/help/external/<ID>` serves a script shell of about 160–171 KB with no article text, on every host tried (`.com`, `.co.uk`, sellercentral-europe, `.in`, `.com.au`, `.sg`, `-japan`, `.ae`). The help hub's content call is a `POST` to an Amazon host; the office ruled on 2026-11-10 (brief 307) that it stays off limits, and brief 548 allows no call to an Amazon host beyond public pages.

**The breach in round 1, and what follows from it.** In round 1, after the office's ruling of 2026-11-10 had closed the help hub's content call, three of the eighteen research and verification agents called it or its siblings. They were found afterwards from their own transcripts [command-verified]:
- The `areas/customer-returns.md` researcher read every Seller Central help page it cites through `POST …/help/hub/mons-api/GetHelpTopic`, on several store hosts.
- That file's verifier re-read the same pages the same way.
- The `method/filing-and-conduct.md` researcher probed other `mons-api` paths by plain GET (`GetBootstrapData` among them) while looking for a readable route. It took no text into the file.

No agent called a Selling Partner API host, signed in or read a seller's data. Round 2 was told the route is closed, and none of its agents called it. Those reads are withdrawn. Every fact that rested only on them is held out of every skill: it is listed in § 11 by position and page id, without its text, and no position anywhere in this tree may be built on it. Other files met the same readings and declined them by name: `areas/value-and-reversals.md` (the live US wording of G200213130, customer-returns RT42), `areas/removals-disposals.md` (a newer reading of the live US removals-claims page) and `method/sp-api-claims-surface.md` (G200732720, G200453320, G200213130). One trace remains inside a live position: `method/filing-and-conduct.md` FL4 states the customer-return route twice, and its second statement is RT41's reading through the closed route; that half is held too (§ 11).

**How a help-page fact reached the tree instead.** Each such fact names the route it came by:

- Amazon staff and News posts on Seller Forums that restate the page, read by plain GET;
- Amazon's policy PDFs on m.media-amazon.com, rendered page by page so struck and inserted text are told apart (a text extraction merges them; value-and-reversals VR28);
- Amazon's Chinese restatements on gs.amazon.cn;
- the listing playbook's read of 2026-10-14 (`cx-listing-playbook` `research/angles/returns.md`), cited as that seat's read and that seat's date, a route the brief names as permitted. That seat's reads of G200453320 and G200379860 were themselves made through the content call before the ruling; customer-returns records this as a remaining doubt;
- a third party's verbatim copy (Hopted, Gorilla ROI, Openbridge, PPC Land, LianLian), tagged as a copy;
- search-result text, tagged as such and never alone under a position.

**The store a forum post was issued for.** A thread opens on every forum host, so the host says nothing about its store. The post object carries its own `marketplaceId`, right after `"contentMetadata":{"mentions":{}}` and before `"pinnedToCategory"`; that value stays the same whichever host serves the thread. fc-lost-damaged § 2a checked the reading on four posts whose store is fixed by their own words (UK title and £ figures, German text, £ amounts) and holds the pack's post-to-store table. The field's value is [command-verified]; that it names the store a News post governs is a reading, since Amazon documents neither the field nor its meaning. Two limits: a seller's post carries the store the seller posted under, not necessarily the store it is about (c0d6d3da); and one News text is issued as a separate thread per store, sometimes in the same second (81c3235d and 6605eede, 0.66 s apart), with different values in each copy.

**Tags.** Each position ends with the tag of its weakest load-bearing source:

- **[read in official docs]**: Amazon's own page, staff or News post, model, PDF or developer document, read on the date given by the route named; **[read in official docs, via X]** when the words reached the tree through a third party's quotation;
- **[command-verified]**: something run on the seat's machine: a grep of the models clone, a parse of a fetched forum page's embedded data, a render of an Amazon PDF;
- **[opinion]**: a named practitioner, vendor or seller;
- **[research]**: published research (recovery-economics only);
- **[inferred]**: the seat's reading of cited sources;
- **seat's own**: no source; it enters no skill (§ 10).

Route marks inside a source: (News) a Seller Forums News post; (staff) a reply by an `*_Amazon` moderator; (PDF) an Amazon PDF on m.media-amazon.com; (sell.amazon) a sell.amazon.* page; (SP-API docs) developer-docs.amazon.com; (listing pack's read); (copy).

**Date read.** 2026-11-13 for every page, unless a row of a file's *Pages read* says otherwise.

**Positions are facts, not instructions.** Under decision D233 each is a definition, a rule of Amazon's, a dated deadline, a pitfall of the data, a measured effect, or a fact about a named tool or practitioner. None says when to ask the seller, sets an order of work, a threshold of ours or a ceiling on what cx may read or change. Where practitioners differ on a method, the files set the methods side by side and pick none. A value that differs by store or year is never a position's substance: the position states the rule and where the value is read at run time, and the value sits in the file's *Values that move*, gathered here in § 5.

**What this synthesis read.** Each file's *Positions*, *Values that move*, *Corrections to other packs*, *Seat's own*, *Held*, *Open questions*, *Unread pages*, *Verification* and *Revision* in full, and the body sections a position needed; the finance pack's `research/README.md`, its `areas/refunds-reimbursements-adjustments.md` position list and its released skill `skills/refunds-and-reimbursements/` (`SKILL.md` and six references); the stock pack's `research/README.md` and its references `skills/stock-inbound/references/shortages.md` and `skills/stuck-stock/references/stranded.md`. It reopened no page and changed no file but this one. Neither released pack is changed: § 3 lists what each would take.

### Follow-ups each pack would take

Collected from every *Corrections to other packs* entry of the nine files (entries withdrawn by their seats are left out; customer-returns withdrew its corrections 2, 3 and 6 for resting on the barred route). Nothing here is changed by this research.

**Finance pack (`cx-amazon-finance-playbook`).**

1. **Store of each News post** (P-ref-13, P-ref-16, § E2, and the released skill line "no page says which store each addresses"). Each post names its store in its own `marketplaceId`: 81c3235d is the US, 6605eede Canada, d0fc997e the UK, 1101821c Sweden, d8f9a56c Australia, and the 2024-08-06 window set is three threads for the UAE (96a05f4a), Saudi Arabia (c6da88ce) and Egypt (e62a8ace). So 2024-11-01 is the US and Canada automation date and 2025-01-15 the UK's. Evidence: the threads read raw 2026-11-13; fc-lost-damaged § 2a, FC41; inbound-shortfalls correction 1; removals-disposals correction 1; recovery-economics corrections 3 and 4; value-and-reversals correction 2.
2. **The US customer-return window** (P-ref-16, § F). P-ref-16 omits 81c3235d, the US issue of 2024-07-24: "between 60-120 days … Claims must not be submitted before 60 days". The 45–105 days is the Canada, UK, Sweden and Australia posts' figure. Evidence: 81c3235d (posted 2024-07-24T19:49:56.298Z). customer-returns correction 1; removals-disposals correction 1.
3. **P-tool-22 is wrong on two counts.** "Amazon's US notices give 45–105 days": the US post gives 60–120. It calls d8f9a56c (effective 2025-10-15) "US"; its post object carries Australia's id. Evidence: recovery-economics correction 4.

P-ref-14's 2025-03-31 rests on the US postponement ed8abc33; the UK has its own postponement 0252e05d (2025-02-28) and Canada its own 2024-12-18 post da761b17; the US 2024-12-18 post is 68864b6f. 5. **UK automation is contested by Amazon itself** (P-ref-13). Evidence: customer-returns correction 4.

## 4. What this pack adds past the two packs

The finance pack's released `refunds-and-reimbursements` skill states reimbursement as money: scope, automation, sourcing-cost value, cash or units, reversals, the windows as conflicting dated facts, and the report's columns. The stock pack states units: states, the ledger, inbound as a position, stuck stock; its `shortages.md` says "The claim itself is outside this pack". What this tree adds is the claim itself.

**The finding.** Amazon's claim rules are per store, and Amazon says which store in each post's own data. Read by the post object's `marketplaceId` (fc-lost-damaged § 2a, FC45–FC47), the 2024–2025 automation and window posts are store-by-store issues of one text with different values, not one rule restated: the US and Canada automation date is 2024-11-01, the UK's 2025-01-15, and no automation post was found for any other store (FC3, FC4); the US customer-return window was posted as 60–120 days, the Canada, UK, Sweden and Australia ones as 45–105 (RT5); the "90 days of initial claim" re-evaluation rule is Australia's (VR20). Amazon also contradicts itself inside one store: the UK staff post of 2026-04-06 says UK warehouse-lost claims are manual, "disabled since August 2022", against three UK News posts (FC5, EC27); the US staff post of 2026-08-13 gives 18-month removal and customer-return ends that match the pre-2024 page, not the 2024 posts (RT6, RM10). For pre-order losses the value fell with the 2025 move to sourcing cost (EC8), so what is left to claim, and what it is worth, depends on the kind, the store and the date.

## 6. Roles

The door's application is taken to hold five roles: Product Listing (PL), Pricing (PR), Brand Analytics (BA), Finance and Accounting (FA) and Amazon Fulfillment (AF). Roles below are the ones Amazon names on *Role Mappings for SP-API Operations* (`https://developer-docs.amazon.com/sp-api/docs/role-mappings`, updated 2026-10-28), *Role Mappings for SP-API Types* (`…/role-mappings-for-types`, 2026-09-09), *FBA Reports* (2026-10-13), *Returns Reports* and *Performance Reports* (2026-10-28), the *Support API* page (2026-09-30) and the *Data Kiosk* pages, all read 2026-11-13 (sp-api-claims-surface §§ 1–2, SU1–SU7, SU47–SU51, SU59). A read needs at least one named role; a `403` is Amazon's refusal for a role the application lacks (the door). That the reports answer on Pricing without Amazon Fulfillment is read in Amazon's documents, not yet seen on a live account (finance P-ref-28).
