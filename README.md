cx reads your Amazon reports to check what Amazon may owe you: units lost or damaged in its warehouses, shipments received short, removals and returns that never came back, fees charged wrongly, and reimbursements paid low. For each, it says whether Amazon already paid and where your store's window is stated. You file claims in Seller Central: cx cannot open a case or send evidence.

Some things you can ask:

- Did every unit from last month's removal orders come back?
- Does Amazon pay for units lost in its warehouse without a claim?
- Can filing many claims put my account at risk?

## What it uses

- **Your Amazon seller account:** cx reads your inventory ledger, reimbursements, returns, removals, shipments and fees there.
